Chapter 2Clip 02

Two wrecks, two free weapons

Permit Keys · Mariana County

Both weapons are available from Chapter 1. In Chapter 2, use any available boat to reach the islands.

  1. Start at the narrow southern island north of Abismo Key, east of Southwind airport. Beach beside the wooden spy vessel on its southern shore.
  2. Board the wreck, open the small deck hatch and take the suppressed Assassin Nipper .38. Equip it to unlock the variant.
  3. Boat north to the second sandy island. Walk to the smaller, tilted wreck where the sand meets the mangroves. Look down on the leaning side of the hull for the Smuggler Gorse 425 shotgun.

Evidence. The two markers in the footage point to different islands. The pistol is in the first boat’s hatch. The second pickup prompt appears from the sand beside the other hull.

Chapter 2Clip 03

Crab research outpost

Pelican Valley · Gullet Marsh

The computer, safe and dock shack are accessible in early free roam. Bring a boat and a stash bag for the marked bills.

  1. Find the research house on the small swamp island northwest of Key Lento. Turn on the generator on its outside wall.
  2. Inspect the bedroom floor for CLEAVES. Enter it at the computer. Follow the on-screen escape prompt during the crab encounter.
  3. Read the specimen sheet by the crab tanks. Specimen 0036 succeeded. Double 36 and enter 0072 at the house safe. Take the cash and read the emergency protocol slip.
  4. Follow the walkway to the waterside shack. Enter 0212, take the Crabshack VF5 by the body and collect the marked bills. Take the bag to a fence that shows a sell prompt.

Evidence. The clip shows a $10,000 sell prompt at Brian’s Lockup and captions the house cash at $1,000. Other runs report different amounts. Sources also disagree about when a fence buys marked bills. The Chapter 3 buyer Yasmani is documented after Boobie Time. The weapon pickup can be done earlier.

Chapter 2Clip 06

Gold Marabunta Grande KZ-59

Nova Auto Junkyard · Hamlet

Documented as a Chapter 2 operation. The creator’s claim that the pickup works at every story stage lacks a matching early save.

  1. Go to the M7 junkyard beside the highway in Hamlet. Find the yellow office and the large blue shipping container opposite it.
  2. Use the yard entrance or the low wall shown in the clip. Under the awning on the north side, take the rifle from the workbench beside the container.
  3. For the other published valuables, check the office safe and adjoining garage. The Albany Teniente Manana’s plate gives the safe code, 7777. The same clue is on the wall behind the safe.

Evidence. The inventory identifies the rifle as Marabunta Grande KZ-59. The yard’s safe and car are independently documented additions to the clip’s rifle route.

Chapter 2Clip 04

Black Eagle warehouse rifle

Black Eagle Ammu-Nation · Balsero

Use Lucia in free roam during Chapter 2. The filmed route works around the obstacle with a forklift.

  1. Go to the green Black Eagle store in northwest Vice City. Face the shop and enter the warehouse on its right through the loading bay.
  2. Find the cargo truck and the fenced enclosure at the back. Use the in-game forklift to lift the blocking pallets and back them out. With Lucia, a reported alternative is the gap between the boxes and chain-link fence.
  3. Walk into the cage. Check the launcher pickup and take the suppressed Black Eagle Import M306 TMR from the open case on the table.

Evidence. The gun prompt says M306 TMR. The clip also shows an AT launcher. The collection route is corroborated. Whether Jason can use exactly the same entry on every save remains unverified.

Chapter 2Clip 16

Sunrise Motel arsenal and gold card

Sunrise Motel · Bayside Drive, Tequesta

An early free-roam pickup. The route needs no chapter-specific tool. Chapter 1 is the creator’s claimed minimum.

  1. Find the motel courtyard on Bayside Drive. Go around the back to the single-car garage on the south side. Lift the roll-up door.

  3. Use the safe-opening interaction at the shelf. Take the cash and the gold Anthony Herndon card from the Los Santos Corkers set.

Evidence. The frame at 00:37 identifies Anthony Herndon. The close-up and card-location guides agree on the gold version. The footage shows a dial interaction at this safe.

Chapter 2Clip 13

The Final Flush stamina upgrade

Corsair Key → Tequesta → Gloriana Key

The quest can start in Chapter 1. The diner meeting is at night.

  1. In the only restroom building on Corsair Key, use WAiNK on the No Flushers QR poster in the men’s room. Reply TRUTH. An independently documented bonus card is by the sink mirror.
  2. Follow the text to Frank’s Waffles in Tequesta at night. Sit with Dr. Merle near the restrooms and choose the code from the message, 00-00-02.
  3. Drive his van by following his spoken directions to the Gloriana Key sewage plant. Keep watch at the gate, follow him inside and go up the metal stairs.
  4. At the upper panel, turn the lights green and pull the lever. Follow the new passage, use the pump control for Merle, then enter the chamber yourself. Look for the maximum-stamina notification.

Evidence. The reward screen at 01:01 confirms an increase to maximum stamina. The sources give no numeric size for the increase.

Chapter 2Clip 12

Russo’s two bunkers

Downtown → Little Cuba → Sharktooth Island → Big Pine

The mystery is reported from Chapter 1. This route follows the clues through both bunkers.

  1. Meet José Bermúdez at the Friends of Freedom memorial on Catalan Boulevard in Downtown. Visit his Cuban-flag apartment near the Little Cuba mod shop on Northwest 4th Avenue. Examine the clues and play the recording.
  2. Go to Sharktooth Island just north of Waning Sands. Use 010317 at the first bunker. Restore power. Search the president’s desk, inspect the cigar and answer the phone. Follow the beeping and listen through the tape.
  3. Read Protocol Two in the desk. Its order gives safe code 2975. Take Russo’s 1922 pistol and inspect the back of the photo for OBSESS.
  4. West of Big Pine, south of the highway Ammu-Nation in North Mariana, find the keypad on a tree beside the ground hatch. Enter 085355. Restore power, follow the second recording, crouch under the half-open shutter and press the red control.
  5. Take the Cold War 556 rifle from the hangar before driving out the Hauners Ridgway tank. Return to this bunker for another tank if you lose it.

Evidence. The tank respawns at the bunker. Beebom reports that it cannot be registered as a personal vehicle. The clip’s “indestructible” title and a guaranteed wanted level are unverified.

Chapter 2Clip 01

Paul Walker’s green Penumbra FF

Vespucci Island · central villa

Moses can register the tracker-free car in Chapter 2. Bring it while your first free registration is still available, or check the paid price.

  1. Reach Vespucci, the middle Rialto Island. At the two central crossroads, use the villa directly north. Walk to its west-side entrance.
  2. Use the side door beside the garage, disable the security panel on the left and open the garage from inside.
  3. Use a Slim Jim or the game’s window-and-hotwire option. Verify the green Maibatsu ’97 Penumbra FF and HAD YOU plate. Opening the trunk triggers the optional snake encounter shown in the clip.
  4. Finish the Chapter 2 mission Moses. Take the car to Watkins Auto Parts, respray it and choose Register. Dexerto reports a $6,750 standard fee. Check the amount on your own screen.

Evidence. The green tribute and the red Royal Ruby Emerus both sit on Vespucci Island, at separate properties. The movie nickname “Eclipse” refers to the Penumbra FF.

Chapter 2Clip 10

Vespucci Mansion, rifle and red Emerus

Neptune Circle · Vespucci Island

The Royal Ruby is documented as a tracker-free early-car option. After Moses opens in Chapter 2, a free first registration can cover it. The first possible chapter for the full robbery remains uncertain.

  1. Go to the walled mansion just south of the main crossing on Neptune Circle. The covered parking bay is beside the driveway on the south side. This is a separate property from the green Penumbra villa.
  2. For the indoor loot, go over the wall left of the main gate. Reach the upper terrace, use the side door, then enter the upstairs gun room at the back.
  3. Take the Kingpin Heathen assault rifle from the wall and the body armor beside it. Use the in-game dial interaction on both safes. One is beside the balcony window, the other on the shelving across the room.
  4. Return to the driveway, open the gate by hand and take the red-and-black Progen Royal Ruby Emerus. Clip 05 starts with this same car. After the Chapter 2 mission Moses, respray this tracker-free car at Watkins Auto Parts and choose Register.

Evidence. Independent runs show different cash totals from the two safes. Both use the dial interaction. GTA6Heists recorded a $29,900 normal registration quote for the red Royal Ruby. The purple Chatfield Emerus has its own scan in Clip 09.

Chapter 2Clip 18

Dom’s Charger and red Chevelle tributes

Grayson · Sid Thompson’s property / western Capri · villa garage

Both are physical car spawns. The Slim Jim is available in Chapter 2. Read the vehicle scan to choose the appropriate registration service.

  1. For the Charger, go northeast of the Grayson town label, near the tracks by San Malo Bay. Behind Sid Thompson’s wooden house, look for the orange-and-white awning and the rusty blue supercharged Bravado DriveShaft Gauntlet.
  2. Enter the car and take it to the Port Gellhorn mod shop. For the film look, select black gloss, remove the stripes and restore the engine. The clip quotes $400, $50 and $200 for those three changes.
  3. For the red car, go to the house on western Capri’s island residential loop. Look for the supercar in the driveway and lift the garage door. Inside is the striped red Declasse ’74 Vamos. Use a Slim Jim or the in-game window option.
  4. Scan each car before registering. The Gauntlet shop frame shows $23,750 registration and $5,805 sale. The Vamos shows $6,000 registration, $1,600 sale, no security and no tracker.

Evidence. The rounded $24,000 caption is $23,750 on the shop panel. The “after the credits” line refers to Dom’s red Chevelle in the film. Dexerto’s GTA route leads straight to the Capri garage. The separate Grayson Vamos request is a different car job.

Chapter 3Clip 17

Vanessa’s pink Growler and free garage

Bayside Apartments → Del Rio Boulevard · La Perle

Missable. Start after Welcome to Vice City and claim the car before starting The Cartel Heist at the end of Chapter 3.

  1. In Boobie’s Bayside apartment, examine the framed dog photo by the kitchen for Muffins. In the bedroom, inspect both sides of the notebook for Queen and the class year 15.
  2. Open the bedroom laptop. Select Muffins → Queen → 15. Open WhatUp, read the thread from Unknown and inspect the message with 92665. This sets up the garage interaction.
  3. Go to Del Rio Boulevard in southern La Perle. Across from Twa Ponya Tattoo, walk behind Auntie’s Place. Use 92665 at the keypad beside the pink bow and take the pink Pfister Vanessa’s Growler.
  4. The garage is free. Check your personal-vehicle list and the shop’s Register line before leaving the car. Complete registration if your save still lists it as stolen.

Evidence. Gunsenburner captions the car as already registered and quotes $14,750 resale. GTA BOOM recorded a $29,450 registration fee and a $20,600 sale offer. The garage unlock and code are corroborated. Automatic ownership and a universal sale price are not established.

Chapter 3Clip 05

Gold Lustrous Toros and Gold Rush Tempesta

Casanova Island → Crimson Palms Tower · Washington Beach

Collect the two gold vehicles after Randy gives you Key Cloner in the Chapter 3 mission Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ Gs. The red car at the beginning of this clip is covered under Clip 10.

  1. For the gold SUV, cross east from Vespucci to Casanova Island. On the southern road, find the middle mansion with a U-shaped driveway. Go over the wall and open the garage.
  2. Use WAiNK and the Key Cloner for the Pegassi Lustrous Toros. Follow the key-signal prompts and take the SUV. This is a different model variant from the Charred Toros at La Perle.
  3. For the gold supercar, head to Crimson Palms Tower on northern Washington Beach. Enter from the front, take the ramp on the right, then look under the covered parking area. Use the Key Cloner for the Pegassi Gold Rush Tempesta.
  4. Take one car at a time. Read its tracker and registration quote before leaving it. Return separately for the second vehicle.

Evidence. The clip calls the supercar an Aventador. The guide uses the game’s Gold Rush Tempesta name. Visiting the garage or tower early does not establish that an early save can start the locked cars.

Hog Stars Garage and Devil’s Rope Gauntlet

Acacia Street · Ambrosia

The early pickup route is documented. The earliest story mission required for every item in the garage is unverified. “Final Chapter” is the motorcycle club’s name.

  1. Find Hog Stars Garage on Acacia Street, west of Ambrosia’s main highway. From the exterior ladder, climb to the roof and drop through the open skylight.

  3. Take crystal from the sofa table and loose cash from the round card table. In the office by the Sprunk Light fridge, hold the safe interaction until the ring fills.
  4. Scan the Gauntlet before leaving. Drive it out through the bay. Use the registration service that accepts the tracker reported on your save.

Evidence. The location-specific GAMES.GG walkthrough documents a hold-to-open safe. A generic robbery template suggests a random PIN. Use the interaction on this office safe. The clip includes a nitrous run but gives no readable registration quote.

Casa Guardia and the Nightlight Schafter

Northeast Little Cuba · Vice City

The car and four key valuables are independently corroborated. The creator’s Chapter 1 claim and the earliest appearance of Raul’s robbery lead are unverified.

  4. Find the orange Benefactor Nightlight Schafter behind the property. Use the gate interaction and drive out. The car, rifle, watch and safe contents are the four named key valuables.

Evidence. The filmed car scan shows $8,500 sale and $3,650 registration. “Two bags = $14K” is the creator’s estimate. Independently recorded payouts vary by buyer and haul.

What remains unresolved

Earliest chapter for six robberies
Hog Stars Garage, La Perle Trap House, Chatfield, Watson Bay, Leonida Carrier Warehouse and Casa Guardia all have independently documented pickups. Their earliest repeatable entry or spawn is unresolved. Chatfield and the Carrier safe have documented Chapter 3 runs.
Vanessa’s car ownership and resale
The garage code and mission deadline agree across sources. Gunsenburner reports automatic registration and $14,750 resale. GTA BOOM recorded a $29,450 registration fee and a $20,600 sale offer. Check the vehicle list and the shop on your save.
Cash, buyers and repeatable prices
The crab lockup’s $10,000 prompt is evidence for one sale. The first chapter for selling marked bills to a particular fence, cash totals in the Vespucci and Watson safes, Casa Guardia’s $14,000 estimate and the truck’s ATM returns lack universal values.
Other creator claims
Jason’s access through the Black Eagle cage, a guaranteed six-star response or indestructible tank, the Elegy being the only drift car, and a numeric stamina increase remain unverified. Directions and rewards supported by evidence appear in their individual routes.
Exact map placement
Map links open existing named community markers. The Permit Keys wrecks use two filmed map views. For the gold Tempesta, use Crimson Palms Tower and the covered ramp in Clip 05. The available named pins do not distinguish this spawn reliably.
